**(Applications for this position are closed, as the internship has been filled !)**

I am offering a Master’s (M2) internship position for the year 2025 within my team at EDF R&D (EDF Lab Paris-Saclay).

The main objective of the internship is to develop a numerical methodology aiming at identifying cracks in rotating shafts, via Bayesian inference. In this context, this method would enable to identify the location and the geometry of a crack, from noisy measurements of the dynamic response of a rotor.

The intern will join a team of research engineers within the ERMES department (Electrotechnics and Structural Mechanics) at EDF R&D for a six-month period. The ideal candidate is a final-year engineering student specializing in computational mechanics or numerical methods for modeling, and demonstrating independence, curiosity, and strong motivation. Knowledges of probability theory and Python programming would be an asset.
