I am offering a Master’s (M2) internship position for the year 2027 within my team at EDF R&D (EDF Lab Paris-Saclay).

The internship focuses on uncertainty quantification (UQ) in the numerical simulation of the mechanical behaviour of nuclear reactor fuel assemblies subjected to dynamic loading.
Based on a finite element model using the code_aster solver, the project aims to assess the impact of input parameter variability on quantities of interest derived from dynamic simulations, particularly in the presence of impact phenomena.

The intern will join a team of research engineers within the ERMES department (Electrotechnics and Structural Mechanics) at EDF R&D for a six-month period. The ideal candidate is a final-year engineering student specializing in applied mathematics or numerical methods for modeling, and demonstrating independence, curiosity, and strong motivation.
