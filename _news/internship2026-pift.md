I am offering a Master’s (M2) internship position for the year 2026 within my team at EDF R&D (EDF Lab Paris-Saclay).

The main objective of the internship is to develop a physics-informed Bayesian inference methodology aimed at solving mechanical inverse problems, while incorporating a variable level of confidence in the model’s constitutive equations.

The intern will join a team of research engineers within the ERMES department (Electrotechnics and Structural Mechanics) at EDF R&D for a six-month period. The ideal candidate is a final-year engineering student specializing in applied mathematics or numerical methods for modeling, and demonstrating independence, curiosity, and strong motivation. Strong knowledge of probability theory and Python programming would be an asset.
